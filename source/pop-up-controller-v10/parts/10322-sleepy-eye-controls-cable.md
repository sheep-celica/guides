## Card Summary

Cable assembly for connecting the sleepy eye controls to the Pop-up Controller V10.

## Details

Use this cable to connect the `10305A` Sleepy Eye controls to the `X5` connector of a Pop-up controller V10.
Same connector on both ends. Doesn't matter which end goes to the Pop-up controller and which one goes to the controls.

## Fact Cards

- Use: Sleepy Eye Control-to-controller cable.
- Works with: `10305A` Sleepy Eye Controls and `10300E` Pop-up Controller V10 - Revision E.
- Length: 1.7 meters
- Compatibility: V8 and V10 Controllers
