10305A - Sleepy Eye Controls

Revision A Small slot Sleepy eye mode LEDs
EUR TBD USD TBD

Small-slot panel with a button to toggle Sleepy Eye mode and a switch to adjust the sleepy eye pop-up position. Supports various LED colors.

This panel can fit in any of the four small slots in the car.

The two small slots under the steering wheel may not be suitable for all installations.

Use

Pop-up Sleepy Eye mode control.

Works with

10322B Sleepy Eye Controls Cable and the controller's X5 input.

Install style

Accessory panel control for compatible interior mounting.

Compatibility
