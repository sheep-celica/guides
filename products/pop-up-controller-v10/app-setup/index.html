App Setup Guide

Download the Windows desktop app, connect the controller, and confirm the app can talk to the hardware before you move on to flashing or settings changes.

Before You Start

What You Need

  • A Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC.
  • The latest desktop app release.
  • A USB-C cable that supports data transfer.
  • A Pop-up Controller V10.

Quick Notes

  • A charge-only USB-C cable will not work.
  • Windows SmartScreen may warn you because the app is not code-signed.
  • If that warning appears, use More info and then Run anyway.

Procedure

1

Download the app

Open the desktop app releases page and download the latest release package.

2

Extract the files

Extract the downloaded release before opening the .exe file.

3

Open the extracted .exe

Open the extracted .exe file.

Note

Windows SmartScreen or antivirus software may warn you about the app because it is not code-signed. If Windows shows the protection warning, click More info and then Run anyway.

→
4

Connect the controller

Connect your controller to your computer.

You can connect the controller directly to your PC or while it is installed in the car.

Use a USB-C cable that supports data transfer. A charge-only cable will not work.

Warning

Connecting the USB-C cable while the controller is powered by 12V through the main connector is not recommended and may damage the controller.

5

Find and connect to the controller

Open the desktop app and click Find controller.

Once the controller is found, click Connect.

6

Confirm the controller information appears

Wait a few seconds for the app to detect the controller and update the device information.

If the Controller Is Not Detected

Quick checks

  • Check that the USB-C cable supports data transfer.
  • Reconnect the controller and try Find controller again.
  • Close and reopen the app, then try again.
  • Try different USB ports on your PC.
